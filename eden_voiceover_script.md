# 🎙️ Eden — Voiceover Script
**For:** Promotional Video
**Duration:** ~60–75 seconds
**Tone:** Confident, clear, warm — not salesy
**Audience:** Academy leaders, coordinators, and team members

---

## SCRIPT

---

**[SCENE 1 — OPENING | 0:00–0:08]**
*Show: App logo / name animation*

> "Running an academy isn't just about teaching.
> It's about managing people, tracking progress, and keeping everyone on the same page —
> every single day."

---

**[SCENE 2 — PROBLEM | 0:08–0:16]**
*Show: Messy spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, scattered tools*

> "Most academies are held together by spreadsheets, group chats, and guesswork.
> Eden was built to change that."

---

**[SCENE 3 — INTRODUCE EDEN | 0:16–0:25]**
*Show: Eden dashboard loading, clean UI*

> "Eden is an all-in-one academy management platform —
> built for HQ leaders, coordinators, and members
> to run their entire academy from one place."

---

**[SCENE 4 — FEATURES | 0:25–0:50]**
*Show: Each feature as it's mentioned — quick cuts*

> "Manage your **courses** and track what's being delivered."

*Cut → Courses page*

> "Post **announcements** that reach your whole team instantly."

*Cut → Announcements page*

> "Keep track of **events** and never miss a key date on your calendar."

*Cut → Events / Calendar page*

> "Monitor **attendance**, review **reports**, and stay on top of every student's journey."

*Cut → Attendance / Reports pages*

> "And with a built-in **messaging** system and full **user directory** —
> your team is always connected."

*Cut → Messages / Users page*

---

**[SCENE 5 — ROLES & CONTROL | 0:50–0:58]**
*Show: Settings or user management screen*

> "Every academy is different — so Eden gives leaders full control
> over who sees what, and who can do what."

---

**[SCENE 6 — CLOSING | 0:58–1:10]**
*Show: Logo, app name, clean final screen*

> "No more scattered tools. No more missed updates. No more guesswork.
> Just your academy — running the way it should."

> **"Eden. Built for academies that mean business."**

---

## 📋 PRODUCTION NOTES

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Total Duration** | ~65–75 seconds |
| **Recommended voice tone** | Calm, authoritative, slightly inspirational |
| **Pacing** | Slow and deliberate — let each line breathe |
| **Music suggestion** | Subtle ambient/cinematic background, low volume |
| **Video style** | Screen recording with smooth transitions OR animated mockups |
| **Font on screen** | Match Eden's UI font — clean, modern sans-serif |
