# Eden Design & Branding Specification

This document details the updated branding assets and official color implementation for the **Eden App** ecosystem, specifically highlighting the integration of our new dual-color accent strategy.

For the full design specifications, typography scales, glassmorphism guidelines, and Tailwind presets, please refer to the main [design-system.md](file:///c:/Users/CT/Documents/Applications/eden/design-system.md) document.

## 1. The Dual-Leaf Logo Update

The Eden brand logo has been upgraded from a monochrome style to a premium, multi-color vector identity:

*   **Typography**: Bold serif typeface ("Eden") in Georgia font. In Light Mode, it uses our primary branding green (`#1F7B00`). In Dark Mode, it automatically transitions to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for optimal contrast and readability.
*   **Dual-Leaf Motif**: 
    *   **Primary Leaf (Emerald `#10B981`)**: Represents creative growth, structured learning, and natural harmony inside the arts academy.
    *   **Secondary Leaf (Soft Gold/Orange `#F59E0B`)**: Represents premium governance, zonal leadership, student achievements, and academic excellence.

## 2. Dynamic Color Balance: Green & Orange-Gold

While **green remains the dominant major color** of the Eden platform (ruling backgrounds, solid headers, and major CTAs), the gold/orange accent color is integrated as a high-contrast warm balance across the marketing page and dashboard application.

### A. Dominant Branding (Green Core)
*   **Deep Forest Green (`#14532D`)**: Used for solid buttons, main sidebars, and dashboard layouts.
*   **Emerald Accent (`#10B981`)**: Used for active menu states, positive status badges, and primary interactive links.

### B. High-Contrast Accent (Soft Gold/Orange `#F59E0B`)
The gold/orange accent is applied to signify premium value, alerts, and focal indicators:
*   **Zonal Leadership Badges**: Highlighted with soft gold tags in dashboards.
*   **Notification Indicators**: Small active notification dots and notification drawer highlights.
*   **Achievement & Verification Badges**: Verified teacher and coordinator accounts display gold checks/tags.
*   **Alert & Action Warnings**: Highlighted with gold/amber border highlights.
